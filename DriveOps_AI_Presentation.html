Physical Asset Monitoring

Machine Health & Physics-Based Telemetry Analytics

ISO 10816 COMPLIANT
Centralized Sensor Thresholds

Deterministic Physical Rules

  • Motor Temperature (°C):
    Nominal: <75°C | Warning: ≥85°C | Critical (Emergency Stop): ≥90°C.
    Action: Automated line halt to prevent stator burnout and bearing seizure.
  • Vibration Velocity (mm/s):
    Nominal: <3.0 | Warning: ≥4.5 | Critical: ≥6.0 mm/s.
    Action: Detects mechanical unbalance, rotor resonance, and early bearing race pitting.
  • Hydraulic & Pneumatic Pressure (psi):
    Optimal Window: 70 - 115 psi | Critical Out-of-Spec: <60 or >125 psi.
    Action: Guarantees robotic clamping precision and guards against valve rupture.
  • Power Consumption (kW):
    Warning: ≥48.0 kW | Critical: ≥55.0 kW surge detection.
